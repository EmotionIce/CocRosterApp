Clash of Clans clan family

A clan for every goal. A family to grow with.

TURTLE is a community-run Clash of Clans clan family built to give every player a clan that fits. Returning casual players, active farmers, dedicated war attackers, and top-level competitive players can join through one Discord and progress through an organized family of clans.

Members get clear management, planned CWL lineups, back-to-back wars, side wars during CWL, hero-down wars, friendly-challenge practice, community events, and Gold Pass giveaways. Reliable players can earn opportunities in stronger clans and higher CWL leagues as their skills and accounts grow.

  • Every play styleCasual, returning, farming, war-focused, and competitive players.
  • Organized competitionBack-to-back wars, fair CWL planning, side wars, and hero-down options.
  • Community rewardsGold Pass giveaways, events, support, and friendly challenges.
  • Future opportunitiesProve yourself, develop your account, and move into stronger clans.
